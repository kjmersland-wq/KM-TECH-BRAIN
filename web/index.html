What it is

KM-TECH BRAIN is a text-understanding model built from scratch with PyTorch. You give it labeled examples, it learns your categories, and it classifies new text with a confidence score — all on your own computer.

What it is not

It is not a general chatbot and does not compete with ChatGPT. It is a focused tool: smaller, private, fast, and tuned to the fields you care about.

  • 100% offline
  • Zero external APIs
  • Your data stays yours
  • Norwegian + English

Current focus areas

Music

Blues and roots first, then modern music. Lyrics analysis, metadata and tags, content ideas and categorization of music-related text.

Web

WebAgent

How it works

  1. 1. Train locally

    Add labeled examples to a CSV or JSON file, then train. Early stopping and checkpointing keep the best model.

    python train.py

  2. 2. Predict

    Load the saved .pth and classify any text, one at a time or in batches, with confidence.

    python predict.py "slide guitar in open G"

  3. 3. Use agents

    Agents combine the model with built-in know-how to produce ideas, checklists and analyses.

    python agents.py "SEO checklist for my Next.js shop"
